## Evidence and scope

Read the existing Burnout 2 reference checkout at `/tmp/b3-library-research-burnout2`,
particularly `src/nodebug/CTransmission.cpp` and `include/types/CTransmission.h`, before
writing source. `bo2map.py` proposes `0x1C6F90` as `CTransmission::Update` at low confidence.
Inspection of the original instructions and Ghidra confirms the algorithm: seven forward
ratios plus reverse, final drive, idle/change-up/change-down/max RPM, clutch and shift timers,
rev limiter, random engine variation and torque curve. Callers are `0x1C0F00` and `0x1C4890`.
They are leads for the vehicle driving-control path, not yet adopted Criterion names.

The original provisional `game/unit_001AB120` spans `0x1AB120`–`0x1D2EE0`, so it is too large
to treat as one physics source file. Small text carves now replace parts of it. Remaining
text and every data slice stay in assembly. D6 carves are preserved by `tusplit.py` and
`dataslice.py`, and counted in objdiff's `game` category.

Do not adopt other mapper proposals without inspection: `0x389600`, proposed as a vehicle
update, actually decodes a 16-bit fixed-point value; its neighbors are not established vehicle
physics routines. The broad vehicle-physics mapping around `0x383C10` remains unverified.

## Recovered transmission layout

`c_cpp/include/transmission.h` models a `0x94`-byte prefix, checked by the PS2 compiler.
The first `0x4C` bytes share the beta's ratio/RPM/torque layout; unknown fields stay opaque.
Burnout 3 adds boost fields at `+0x4C`/`+0x50`, moving engine velocity/clutch state to
`+0x54`/`+0x58`/`+0x5C`/`+0x60`. The compact RNG words are at `+0x68`/`+0x6C`, seeded with
`0xFD462907`/`0x02B9D6F8`; update adds `(state << 16)` to the **arithmetic** high-half shift `(s32)state >> 16`,
then performs the two additions. This corrects the initial shorthand description as a rotate:
a logical rotation would change negative-state sequences. Manual/switch state is `+0x70`–`+0x7C`,
current/top gear `+0x80`/`+0x84`,
down/up timers `+0x88`/`+0x8C`, and the vehicle pointer `+0x90`. These offsets come from
Burnout 3 instructions, not the beta's much larger `0x170`-byte object.

## Vehicle-helper evidence and layout

`vehicle_physics.h` is a partial retail view, not a complete class identification. It retains
opaque gaps and neutral names for fields without a verified Criterion identity. It records
the quadword speed sample at `+0xB0`, transform at `+0x204`, flag `+0x210`, body-state pointer
`+0xCC4`, state bytes `+0x1169`/`+0x116A`, boost speed ceiling `+0x13D4`, race-car/resource
pointers `+0x13F4`/`+0x13F8`, transmission `+0x1448`, and deadline `+0x1570`.

- Resource release uses six pointers inside parent `D_01D9C350` at `+0xAE0`, preserving the
  original relocation. Caching the resource before the loop recovered the saved-register
  and loop shape exactly. The pointer is cleared even when no slot matches.
- Collision flags `0x40` or `0x20` select the first transform axis or `D_004B7DA0`;
  otherwise `0x80` selects the third axis, with the second axis as default. The fixed default
  is `D_004B7DB0`. Constant vectors and the downstream `0x1CC580` body stay assembly.
  A native `__int128` copy recovered the original `lq`/`sq`; scalar float copies did not.
- Body state is loaded with `lb` and compared unsigned with two. Negative byte values are
  not eligible. Expressing this as signed state zero or one recovers the original `sltiu at`
  code and all bytes; a direct cast comparison used a scratch register instead.
- Lifecycle address names stay unchanged: this batch does not establish the Criterion
  names of `0x1CEE10`/`0x1CFD20`. All new callees remain preserved assembly.

### Initialization audit (2026-10-10)

`0x1CFD20` is the retail counterpart of B2 `CVehiclePhysics::Init`, not its Reset:
it initializes six wheel slots, clears wheel counts, sets default suspension values,
initializes the embedded collision structure, and sets bounding-volume defaults. Its
caller `0x1C6EF0` then sets wheel counts at +0x1169/+0x116A to four, matching B2's
`CRaceCarPhysics::Init` sequence. The existing `vehicle_reset.cpp` filename is historical;
the function keeps its address-based symbol. Counts are read with signed byte loads in
wheel loops; new interfaces must preserve that signedness.

Retail evidence separates two 0x10-byte suspension records at +0xCA0 and +0xCB0:
attach height +0, damping constant +4 (5000), spring constant +8 (60000), spring length
+0xC (0.2). `0x1CDB70` independently uses +0xCAC/+0xCBC as front/rear spring lengths.
The six wheels start at +0x820 with stride 0xC0. Init writes radius 29 at wheel +0x50,
two unit scale values at +0x6C/+0x70, and clears six matrix pointers at vehicle +0xCC8.
These are retail offsets; B2's wheel/suspension layouts cannot be copied verbatim.

`0x1AD850` (64 bytes) clears type +0x215 and transform +0x204, assigns the shared
triangle cache at `0x66C580`, resets its count, installs triangle/tag buffers at
`0x66C590`/`0x66DD90`, then tail-calls `0x1AD380` (1220 bytes). `0x2BAEE0` (16 bytes)
clears three bytes at embedded-object offsets +0x18/+0x19/+0x1A. The two small helpers are now exact and linked; `0x1AD380` remains assembly.

`0x1AB610` (156 bytes) copies all four transform quadwords to the external matrix
and vehicle +0x70, transposes the three basis vectors there, and sets inverse translation
to the negative transformed position. It is a rigid-transform inverse, not a general
matrix inverse. Nine callers are recorded in `build/d6-oct10-helpers.txt`; the original
VU instructions establish this role even though Ghidra's partial-lane output is misleading.

Read-only Ghidra and original-instruction evidence is in `build/d6-oct10-helpers.txt`
