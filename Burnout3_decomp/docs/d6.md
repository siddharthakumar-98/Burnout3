# D6: Vehicle physics and handling

Started 2026-10-08 on branch `D6`, from `a652d65` (`burnout3-rescope`). D6 is in progress;
the transmission/collision/body-helper batches have seventeen functions in fifteen C++ files,
twelve matched and linked.
Physics stepping, suspension, steering, drift and boost handling are still open. D4/D5 tails
remain recorded in their own milestone logs.

## Evidence and scope

Read the existing Burnout 2 reference checkout at `/tmp/b3-library-research-burnout2`,
particularly `src/nodebug/CTransmission.cpp` and `include/types/CTransmission.h`, before
writing source. `bo2map.py` proposes `0x1C6F90` as `CTransmission::Update` at low confidence.
Inspection of the original instructions and Ghidra confirms the algorithm: seven forward
ratios plus reverse, final drive, idle/change-up/change-down/max RPM, clutch and shift timers,
rev limiter, random engine variation and torque curve. Callers are `0x1C0F00` and `0x1C4890`.
They are leads for the vehicle driving-control path, not yet adopted Criterion names.

The original provisional `game/unit_001AB120` spans `0x1AB120`–`0x1D2EE0`, so it is too large
to treat as one physics source file. Small text carves now replace parts of it. Remaining
text and every data slice stay in assembly. D6 carves are preserved by `tusplit.py` and
`dataslice.py`, and counted in objdiff's `game` category.

Do not adopt other mapper proposals without inspection: `0x389600`, proposed as a vehicle
update, actually decodes a 16-bit fixed-point value; its neighbors are not established vehicle
physics routines. The broad vehicle-physics mapping around `0x383C10` remains unverified.

Initialization's sole direct caller is `0x1C6600`, which passes the transmission at vehicle
`+0x1448` and the vehicle pointer. It seeds the two-word RNG, clears switches/gear/clutch,
scans positive ratios up to gear six, repeats the state reset, clears both shift timers,
stores the physics pointer, and returns one. Preserve the duplicate reset sequence. Unlike
Burnout 2's separate Setup/Reset methods, it does not copy tuning attributes itself, so its
address-based name remains until the retail method identity is established.

The RPM helper's callers are `0x1BED00`, `0x1BFE80`, `0x1D1670`. It computes `speed / divisor`,
then tests forward ratios from gear one while `gear < mnTopGear`; each candidate angular
speed is `finalDrive * (ratio * gearRatio)`. All three callers store `v0` into vehicle
`+0x14C8`, the embedded transmission's current gear (`+0x1448 + 0x80`). The integer return type is confirmed. They pass vehicle `+0x9F0`
as divisor and the `+0xB0` sample's fourth float as speed; the divisor's tuning identity
still needs verification.

## Recovered transmission layout

`c_cpp/include/transmission.h` models a `0x94`-byte prefix, checked by the PS2 compiler.
The first `0x4C` bytes share the beta's ratio/RPM/torque layout; unknown fields stay opaque.
Burnout 3 adds boost fields at `+0x4C`/`+0x50`, moving engine velocity/clutch state to
`+0x54`/`+0x58`/`+0x5C`/`+0x60`. The compact RNG words are at `+0x68`/`+0x6C`, seeded with
`0xFD462907`/`0x02B9D6F8`; update adds `(state << 16)` to the **arithmetic** high-half shift `(s32)state >> 16`,
then performs the two additions. This corrects the initial shorthand description as a rotate:
a logical rotation would change negative-state sequences. Manual/switch state is `+0x70`–`+0x7C`,
current/top gear `+0x80`/`+0x84`,
down/up timers `+0x88`/`+0x8C`, and the vehicle pointer `+0x90`. These offsets come from
Burnout 3 instructions, not the beta's much larger `0x170`-byte object.

## Open matching work

Eight RPM source variants were tested under locked b119 flags. Retained source: 99.13%,
identical instruction/control-flow shape, but ratio and change-up threshold use `f4`/`f2`
where the original uses `f2`/`f4`. Moving the angular-velocity local ahead of the threshold
recovered `f3`; returning the selected gear recovered `v0`. Scratch variants and comparison
output are ignored under `build/d6-rpm-variants/` and `build/d6-rpm-match.txt`.

The original stores an incoming/undefined `f3` if top gear is at most one, and the C draft
has an uninitialized local on that path. Keep this unit unlinked. Verify caller invariants
before linking; do not replace that edge with a made-up zero speed.

The retained torque-update draft is 85.78% after four control-flow/timer variants
and two ratio-address variants. It recovers the complete scalar
algorithm, but is not accepted as behaviorally or byte-exactly verified. Main matching leads:
initial target-speed scheduling and the redundant gear guard, FPR allocation, and distinct
quadword speed-copy stack slots. Separate timer reads reached 85.83% in scratch; the retained byte-view ratio accessor
avoids indexing beyond the standalone reverse-ratio member and scores 85.78%. Deferring the
final random update until the torque expression dropped to 82.78%. Scratch is under
`build/d6-batch-variants/torque/`; retain the separate RNG-before-output sequence.

The retail differences from the checked beta include accelerator floor 0.9 under boost,
100x negative wheel speed in first gear, shorter boosted shift clutch/up timer, a rev-limit
negative accelerator clamped at zero, faster boosted engine-speed ramp, additive idle jitter
with the one-MPH gate, the 0.035-second clutch on return to neutral, and the race-car boost
window/flag torque multiplier below the vehicle's boost-speed ceiling. Callers in `0x1C0F00`
pass accelerator minus brake, wheel angular speed, control bit four, and the boolean test of
race-car `+0x178C == 0`; the resulting torque is stored at vehicle `+0x1520`.

## Vehicle-helper evidence and layout

`vehicle_physics.h` is a partial retail view, not a complete class identification. It retains
opaque gaps and neutral names for fields without a verified Criterion identity. It records
the quadword speed sample at `+0xB0`, transform at `+0x204`, flag `+0x210`, body-state pointer
`+0xCC4`, state bytes `+0x1169`/`+0x116A`, boost speed ceiling `+0x13D4`, race-car/resource
pointers `+0x13F4`/`+0x13F8`, transmission `+0x1448`, and deadline `+0x1570`.

- Resource release uses six pointers inside parent `D_01D9C350` at `+0xAE0`, preserving the
  original relocation. Caching the resource before the loop recovered the saved-register
  and loop shape exactly. The pointer is cleared even when no slot matches.
- Collision flags `0x40` or `0x20` select the first transform axis or `D_004B7DA0`;
  otherwise `0x80` selects the third axis, with the second axis as default. The fixed default
  is `D_004B7DB0`. Constant vectors and the downstream `0x1CC580` body stay assembly.
  A native `__int128` copy recovered the original `lq`/`sq`; scalar float copies did not.
- Body state is loaded with `lb` and compared unsigned with two. Negative byte values are
  not eligible. Expressing this as signed state zero or one recovers the original `sltiu at`
  code and all bytes; a direct cast comparison used a scratch register instead.
- Lifecycle address names stay unchanged: this batch does not establish the Criterion
  names of `0x1CEE10`/`0x1CFD20`. All new callees remain preserved assembly.

## Collision/cache and body-helper batch (2026-10-09)

Seven more retail functions are recovered: four exact functions are linked in three units,
and three collision drafts remain unlinked. This adds **496 exact instruction bytes**;
the full batch recovers **1,580 instruction bytes** as source. The original SHA-1 still matches.
The six new text carves leave data ownership and all remaining physics functions in assembly.

The Burnout 2 `CCollidingBody::CacheWorldTriangles`, `_CollectTrianglesCallback`,
`CacheFriendlyTriangle`, and `CCollidingBodyWorldTriangleData` were checked first. They
establish the query/cache role but do not supply retail layouts or a one-to-one method name.
The beta keeps 64 triangles inline; retail uses a count and two pointers at vehicle `+0x200`:
count `+0`, 0x40-byte triangle storage `+4`, separate 16-bit tags `+8`. Capacity is 96.
A query triangle has its normal at `+0x10`, three inline vertices at `+0x20`, and a surface
pointer at `+0x60`; the surface tag is at that object's `+4`. Copies preserve all four words
of each vector, including the normal's fourth word. Compiler checks guard the cache,
transform, optional-triangle, transmission and race-car-state offsets.

- `0x1AB090` appends while the shared byte `D_004E2704` is clear and signed count is below
  96; failure sets that byte and returns zero. Retained match is **99.67%**. The two load/store
  pairs for vertex zero and normal use exchanged quadword registers/order; eight source-form
  variations plus the final register-qualified check did not close this. The function is
  still assembly in the executable. Scratch: `build/d6-collision-variants/append/`.
- `0x1C5330` ignores low-byte tags `0x23`/`0x22` or tag bit `0x1000`. For tags `0x15`–`0x20`,
  it also ignores a triangle when the VU0 dot product of speed sample and normal exceeds
  `0.5`. The subsequent normal-y threshold is retail **-0.7**, verified from immutable
  `0x4E0DC4`; do not copy the beta's unrelated wall-height threshold. All other triangles
  pass to the append routine. Retained match is **56.84%**: the vector operations are
  recovered, but the cold block arrangement, boolean temporaries and stack frame still
  differ (0x20 versus retail 0x70). Seven expression/inline variants are recorded under
  `build/d6-collision-variants/filter/`; do not repeat them without a new scalar/vector
  wrapper model. The macro blocks need `asm volatile` to keep the retail GPR/VU bridges.
- `0x1C5080` queries around transform translation with radius `VU0 length(+0x1D0) + travel`.
  Travel is speed-sample w times `D_0052267C`; when race-car `+0x1914` is nonzero, positive
  step count multiplies it again, while nonpositive count uses zero. Preserve VU0 w-only
  products, square root and final sphere-w update. The query clears both cache count and
  overflow byte, reads signed world index `+0x217`, stores signed unit result `+0x216`, and
  supplies the filter callback. Six optional triangles at `+0x11D0` are appended only if
  flag `+0x1351` is set and count is **strictly below 90**. The first two get tag `0x26`,
  the last four `0x1A`; a full cache clears the flag. Retained match is **73.16%**, up from
  58.30% after using a three-vector aggregate copy and a local tag pointer. Next leads are
  the original 0x120 stack frame versus 0x50, separate speed-copy temporaries, scalar/VU
  wrapper boundaries and scheduling around the query. Logs: `build/d6-query-{1,2}.txt`.
- `0x1ABE40` is the activation helper called by `0x1CBC70`. The latter's incoming second
  argument is passed through to activation, even though Ghidra initially omits it from
  the caller's inferred signature. Registration calls `0x354CF0` with each of
  `D_0064A8F0`/`D_0064B940`, clears `+0x116B` and stores translation y at `+0x116C`.
  The inlined quadword translation getter preserves the 0x40 frame and register form.
- `0x1CBCE0` uses body-state flag `+0x1023` and the pointer at `+0x1024`; its first signed
  byte is the loop count. It sets the flag before allocation, so failure does not clear
  the one-time flag. `0x1CBD80` changes body-state `+0x4B2 + index` from two to three
  before allocation and restores two on failure. A successful part goes to `0x333990`
  and the `0x27D240` position callback with 100.0. Existing `0x1CBE50` supplied the matching
  integer-address indexing form; retaining the failure-path return recovered the epilogue.
  The body-part helpers remain address-named, and their downstream implementations stay assembly.
